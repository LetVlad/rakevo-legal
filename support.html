Common questions

  • Can't sign in? Make sure you're using the email address you registered with. If you forgot your password, use "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen.
  • Managing your cars, expenses or reminders? Open the app and go to your car's screen to add or edit vehicles, expenses, and renewal reminders.
  • Messaging another driver? Rakevo connects drivers by license plate — phone numbers are never shared.
  • Want to delete your account? See our account deletion page.
